# Research artifacts

Committed artifacts are compact, decision-relevant research records. Full deterministic campaign output is regenerated by `blowup-lab` in CI and uploaded as a workflow artifact.

## Evidence rule

Generated JSON/CSV is evidence of what an evaluator computed, not evidence that the underlying mathematical conjecture is true. The corresponding source lock and claim boundary govern interpretation.
